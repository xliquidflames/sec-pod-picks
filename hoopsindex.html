How the Podium Works:
Cousins are ranked purely by their individual correct and incorrect picks added up for the season. The cousins with the most correct picks are first, fewest losses breaking that. Anyone sharing the exact same record shares the spot, shown as a tie. You can tap the row to open it and see every name. Ties aren't something this tries to break, especially early in a season.
Why doesn't everyone's win total match the number of games played? They haven't played every week. We don't force you to have played every week to have a record at all but we can't have someone riding a single undefeated week to the end of the season. For example, someone who only picked one week and is 40-0 at the end of the season has 100% accuracy. But someone who is 450-60 has more correct picks. So we are keeping it purely about the total correct picks, not accuracy.

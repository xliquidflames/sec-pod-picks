How the Podium Works:
Ranked purely by cumulative correct and incorrect picks. Whoever has the most correct picks is first, fewest losses breaking that. Anyone sharing the exact same record shares the spot, shown as a tie. You can tap the row to open it and see every name. Ties aren't something this tries to break, especially early in a season.
Why doesn't everyone's win total match the number of games played? Not everyone plays every week. A record only grows once someone actually submits that week's picks, but we can't have someone riding a single undefeated week to the end of the season. For example, someone who only picked one week and is 16-0 at the end of the season has 100% accuracy. But someone who's played most of the season with a much bigger record, even with some losses mixed in, still has more total correct picks. So we are keeping it purely about the total correct picks, not accuracy.
